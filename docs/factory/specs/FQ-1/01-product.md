# FQ-1 Gate 1 - Product: About page

Source: issue #1 "Add an About page".

## The problem
A visitor who lands on the site has no way to find out what it is or why it exists. They
see a template home page and nothing that tells them what they are looking at.

## Success
- A visitor can reach a page titled "About" from the main navigation on any page, in one click.
- The page has a short paragraph (2-4 sentences) saying what the app is.

## Announcement
> **New: About page.** Wondering what this site is? Click **About** in the top navigation
> for a short description of the app and what it is for.

## Mockup
`mockups/about.html`: nav with Home, Privacy, About; heading "About"; one short paragraph.

## Deliberately not doing
- No team, contact, or biography sections.
- No images, logo, or marketing copy.
- No content management; the text is fixed.
- No localization.
- No changes to the home page or footer.
- No redesign of the navigation.
